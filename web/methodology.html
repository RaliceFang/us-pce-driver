PCE 物價貢獻度計算方法

主圖使用 BEA 官方價格月貢獻度。產品細項則使用官方價格與支出資料重建，並明確標示估算。所有貢獻的單位均為百分點（pp）。

1. 資料與定義

BEA 表用途
2.8.8（月）官方價格月增率貢獻度，發布至小數兩位。不是 2.8.2 的實質消費成長貢獻。
2.8.4 / 2.8.5（月）官方主要細項價格與名目支出，用於交叉驗證。
2.4.4U（月）產品細項季調價格指數，2017=100。
2.4.5U（月）產品細項季調年率名目支出，單位百萬美元。占比中年率倍數相消。

月增率 MoM 為本月對上月，未年率化。年增率 YoY 為本月對去年同月，使用同一組季調 PCE 價格指數。PCE 可修訂整段歷史，與原 CPI 專案的未季調年增率不同。

食物僅含供居家消費的食物與飲料（含酒精飲料）。餐飲服務屬於核心服務。能源包括汽油及其他能源商品、電力與天然氣服務。住宅包含租戶租金、自有住宅設算租金、農場住宅與集體住宅，不包含所有公用事業費用。

2. 官方拆解

16 主要細項直接讀取 2.8.8 的互斥列：耐久財四項、非耐久財四項、家庭服務七項、非營利機構淨消費一項。四大類與六細項由官方列做加減：

食物 = line 9
能源 = line 27
核心商品 = line 2 − line 9 − line 11
核心服務 = line 13 − line 27 + line 11
能源服務 = line 27 − line 11
住宅 = line 29
不含住宅核心服務 = 核心服務 − line 29

2.8.8 的「核心 PCE 貢獻」不是核心指數的漲幅。圖中的核心折線直接取核心價格指數計算，不能與整體貢獻相加。官方貢獻已四捨五入，各項加總與以三位小數指數計算的總體漲幅會不同，差額單獨顯示。

3. 年增率的逐月鏈接

不能把十二個月的月貢獻直接相加，也不能用去年單一權重乘細項年漲幅。由價格水準差額的望遠鏡加總：

cᵧᵢ(t) = Σⱼ₌ₜ₋₁₁…ₜ cₘᵢ(j) × I(j−1) / I(t−12)
YoY(t) = 100 × [I(t) / I(t−12) − 1]
差額ᵧ(t) = YoY(t) − Σᵢ cᵧᵢ(t)

I 是整體 PCE 價格指數。每個月的月貢獻換成相對於去年同月的價格水準差額，再加總。這是本專案採用的年度分配方法，不稱為 BEA 官方年度貢獻。缺任何日曆月份或必要值時不跨月冒充單月資料。

4. 產品細項的 Fisher 重建

PCE 使用鏈式 Fisher 指數，不使用 CPI 的年度固定籃子。對相鄰月份，取細項价格相對 rᵢ = pᵢ(t)/pᵢ(t−1)，兩期名目支出占比 s₀ᵢ、s₁ᵢ。對完整互斥的細項集合，重建：

L = Σᵢ s₀ᵢ rᵢ
P = 1 / Σᵢ(s₁ᵢ / rᵢ)
F = √(L × P)
cᵢ = 100 × P / (F + 1) × (s₀ᵢ + s₁ᵢ/rᵢ) × (rᵢ − 1)
Σᵢ cᵢ = 100 × (F − 1)

此公式由 F²−1 = P(L−1)+(P−1) 推導，精確加總到「重建的」Fisher 漲幅。與 BEA 整體指數仍可能不同，原因包括公布精度、聚合階層與官方底層資料。差額保留，不按權重偷偷分攤。逐月支出集合須先與官方總支出核對，僅允許各列百萬美元整數的四捨五入差異，才對完整集合的占比做微小正規化。

產品階層使用 BEA 縮排與列號，預設拆至第 4 層。某分支沒有完整歷史時保留有資料的母項。淨境外商品支出與淨旅遊支出沒有價格指數，改用支出及扣減項各自的官方價格。標為「Less:」的分支按負支出計算；非營利機構銷售收入同樣扣除。重複的序列代碼不可去重，因為它們可以出现在不同會計位置。

細項表中的母項是選定最底層貢獻的加總，不是重新套母項價格計算。母項與子項不可同時加總。占比是當月名目支出占比，估算貢獻使用相鄰兩個月的占比。負占比是會計扣減，細項自身漲幅維持原方向。

5. 驗證與更新

每次更新比對兩份 BEA 檔的發布日期及資料月份；價格與名目支出在主要細項逐月逐列對帳。檢查支出覆蓋、負號、父子加總、年鏈接、所有資料長度及差額。計算測試包含變動籃子、通縮、扣減、缺值與日曆斷點。

本次資料與驗證數字見 validation.json。1990–2026 年 8 月首次建立時，73 細項支出覆蓋約 99.9998%–100.0001%；與官方主要細項的單月貢獻最大差異 0.0133 pp，重建總體最大月差額 0.0409 pp。官方 16 細項最大月差額 0.0347 pp、年鏈接差額 0.1189 pp。這是同一發布版本不同官方表的驗證，未另與 Bloomberg 或其他獨立供應商對帳。

GitHub Actions 在平日更新，下載整份最新版本、重算歷史、通過檢查後才提交並發布。下載失敗或兩份 BEA 檔版本不同時停止；不默默用舊快取冒充新資料。設定 GitHub Pages 為 Actions 後即可使用。

PCE contribution methodology

The main chart uses BEA's published monthly price contributions. Detailed products use reconstructed Fisher estimates. Contributions are percentage points (pp).

1. Sources and definitions

Table 2.8.8 provides monthly contributions to price changes, rounded to 0.01 pp. Table 2.8.2 concerns real consumption growth and is not used. Tables 2.8.4/2.8.5 validate major-product prices and expenditures. Underlying tables 2.4.4U/2.4.5U provide detailed prices (2017=100) and nominal expenditures (millions of dollars, SAAR).

MoM is not annualized. Both MoM and YoY use seasonally adjusted PCE indexes. The entire historical vintage may be revised. Food is food and beverages purchased for off-premises consumption, including alcohol. Restaurant food is a core service. Energy comprises gasoline and other energy goods plus electricity and gas. Housing comprises actual and imputed rents, farm dwellings and group housing, excluding other utilities.

2. Official monthly breakdowns

The 16 major products use mutually exclusive rows of 2.8.8: four durable goods, four nondurable goods, seven household services, and net NPISH consumption. Four- and six-part breakdowns use sums and differences:

Food = line 9; Energy = line 27
Core goods = line 2 − line 9 − line 11
Core services = line 13 − line 27 + line 11
Energy services = line 27 − line 11
Housing = line 29
Core services excluding housing = core services − line 29

The core contribution in 2.8.8 is not the core inflation rate. The cyan core line is calculated directly from the core price index. Rounded contributions differ from index-derived headline inflation; their residual is displayed separately.

3. Linked yearly contributions

cᵧᵢ(t) = Σⱼ₌ₜ₋₁₁…ₜ cₘᵢ(j) × I(j−1) / I(t−12)
YoY(t) = 100 × [I(t) / I(t−12) − 1]
residualᵧ(t) = YoY(t) − Σᵢ cᵧᵢ(t)

I is the headline price index. This telescoping price-level allocation links the monthly contributions; it is not a simple sum, a fixed base-year weight calculation, or an official BEA yearly contribution series. Missing months or required inputs invalidate the window.

4. Reconstructed detailed Fisher contributions

PCE is a chain-type Fisher index. For price relatives rᵢ=pᵢ(t)/pᵢ(t−1) and nominal shares s₀ᵢ and s₁ᵢ in adjacent months:

L = Σᵢ s₀ᵢ rᵢ; P = 1 / Σᵢ(s₁ᵢ / rᵢ); F = √(L × P)
cᵢ = 100 × P/(F+1) × (s₀ᵢ+s₁ᵢ/rᵢ) × (rᵢ−1)
Σᵢ cᵢ = 100 × (F−1)

The identity follows from F²−1=P(L−1)+(P−1). Contributions add exactly to the reconstructed bilateral Fisher change, which may differ from BEA's full headline index because of precision, aggregation and unavailable underlying inputs. The residual is never redistributed. Only complete nominal partitions passing integer-dollar rounding tolerances are normalized.

The BEA line-number hierarchy is expanded to level 4 by default. A parent is retained when children lack complete history. Net foreign spending without a price index is decomposed into priced spending and deduction branches. Less: branches carry negative expenditures, including NPISH sales receipts. Repeated series codes retain distinct accounting positions. Parent contributions sum the chosen leaves; parents and children must not be added together. Displayed expenditure shares are current-month shares, while estimates use both adjacent months. Own price changes keep their sign even on deduction rows.

5. Validation and updates

Updates require matching release dates and latest months in both source workbooks. Major-product price and expenditure observations are checked across the main and underlying tables. Release checks cover signed spending coverage, hierarchy, yearly linking, array lengths and residuals. Unit tests cover changing quantities, deflation, deductions, missing inputs and calendar gaps.

The current diagnostics are in validation.json. For the initial 1990–August 2026 vintage, 73 leaves cover 99.9998%–100.0001% of expenditures. The maximum monthly leaf-vs-official major contribution difference is 0.0133 pp; the maximum reconstructed headline monthly residual is 0.0409 pp. The official 16-product residual maxima are 0.0347 pp monthly and 0.1189 pp yearly. This validates tables from the same BEA vintage, not independent vendor data or Bloomberg.
